# São Paulo World — showcase page

Static page published at **zrossermcintosh.com/sao-paulo**.

- `sao-paulo/index.html` — the page (no build step)
- `sao-paulo/site_data.json` — snapshot of the tile grid, QA grades, rail lines and street names, generated by `tools/site_build/build_site_data.py` in the SaoPauloWorld project
- `sao-paulo/img/` — city mosaic and renders
- `sao-paulo/driving/` — browser driving test track at **zrossermcintosh.com/sao-paulo/driving**: the OpenRoad Dynamics vehicle core (github.com/ZRosserMcIntosh/Game-Vehicle-Dynamics, MIT) compiled to WebAssembly (`ord.mjs` + `ord.wasm`, rebuilt with `web/build.sh` or `web/build_wasi.sh` in that repo), rendered with three.js (MIT, `driving/vendor/`). Put a Blender export at `driving/models/car.glb` to make it the default car.

Data: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL); GeoSampa, Prefeitura de São Paulo.
- `sao-paulo/glass/` — glass fracture lab at **zrossermcintosh.com/sao-paulo/glass**: the Glass Dynamics fracture core (github.com/ZRosserMcIntosh/Glass, MIT) compiled to WebAssembly (`glass.mjs` + `glass.wasm` + `library.json`, rebuilt with `WASI_SDK=… web/build_wasi.sh sao-paulo/glass` in that repo), rendered with the three.js copy in `driving/vendor/`. Without WebGL it draws a 2D front view (`?flat` forces it). `glass_audio.js` synthesizes the sound from engine events (no audio files); `scene_gt.js` is the armoured-car scenario (`/sao-paulo/glass/?scene=gt` opens straight into it); `scene_house.js` the grenade in a house with twelve tempered windows (`?scene=house`), one engine pane per window.
- `sao-paulo/liquids/` — liquids lab at **zrossermcintosh.com/sao-paulo/liquids**: the Liquid Dynamics fluid core (github.com/ZRosserMcIntosh/liquid, MIT) compiled to WebAssembly (`liquid.mjs` + `liquid.wasm` + `library.json`, rebuilt with `WASI_SDK=… web/build_wasi.sh sao-paulo/liquids` in that repo), rendered with the three.js copy in `driving/vendor/`. A wine glass knocked off a table, a bottle dropped on the floor, a crash dummy shot with green stage blood (any round, as often as you like; an optional ragdoll; glass in front, behind or both, any of the Glass lab's glazings, broken by its `../glass/glass.mjs`, with the spatter landing on its pieces and falling with them), drip and impact-angle cards and a dam break; bloodstain pattern analysis with "strings", and export to glTF (.glb), stains JSON and PLY for Unreal and Unity.
- `sao-paulo/ballistics/` — ballistics range at **zrossermcintosh.com/sao-paulo/ballistics**: the Ballistic Dynamics exterior-ballistics core (github.com/ZRosserMcIntosh/ballistic-dynamics, MIT) with Aviation Dynamics' atmosphere, compiled to WebAssembly (`ballistic.mjs` + `ballistic.wasm` + `library.json`, rebuilt with `WASI_SDK=… web/build_wasi.sh sao-paulo/ballistics` in that repo), rendered with the three.js copy in `driving/vendor/`. 1 km, 1 mile, 2 km and 2 mile shots and more: wind along the range (three stretches, gusts, your call against the truth), temperature, altitude and humidity, spin drift, aerodynamic jump, Coriolis and Eötvös; a scope with a MIL reticle and mirage, a dope card, a slow-motion bullet camera, share links that fire the same shot (`how/` has the equations and sources). A crash dummy behind any of the Glass Lab's glazings hands the arriving round to the Liquids Lab (`liquids/?from=ballistics&…`: speed, mass, diameter, descent and drift), which breaks the glass with Glass Dynamics and plays the impact. The Rosser V8 (`driving/models/car.glb`) can be the target, with the dummy at the wheel behind its armoured windscreen: the range loads `../glass/glass.mjs` and fires the round into the raked pane at its real angle. The trace is the engine's wake (`bd_wake`) drawn as refraction; hit markers are optional.
- `sao-paulo/destruction/` — destruction lab at **zrossermcintosh.com/sao-paulo/destruction**: the Destruction Dynamics playground (github.com/ZRosserMcIntosh/Destruction-Dynamics, MIT), "The Deep End": a fictional 85-storey Balneário Camboriú tower tips into the ocean, raises a surge and settles as a wreck you can swim. `assets/app.js` is the Vite build of `apps/playground` (three.js and Rapier bundled; rebuild with `npm run build` in that repo and copy `apps/playground/dist/assets/index-*.js` over it). `index.html` is the build's page with the site's skin and `<base>`.
