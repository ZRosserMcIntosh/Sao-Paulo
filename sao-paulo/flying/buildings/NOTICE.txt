The building tiles in this folder (b_<i>_<j>.bin.gz, tall.bin.gz, index.json)
are derived from the Overture Maps buildings theme (release 2026-10-21.0):

- OpenStreetMap building footprints and heights (2,070,013 buildings).
- Where no OpenStreetMap building overlaps them, the footprints traced from
  imagery by Google (Google Open Buildings, 2,109,798) and Microsoft
  (Microsoft ML Buildings, 268,171). They carry no heights: each is given the
  median mapped height of the OpenStreetMap buildings of its size around it
  (house-sized ones at most 12 m, others at most 45 m), or one from its
  footprint (4-12 m).

  Building data (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, Microsoft, Google Open
  Buildings; available under the Open Database Licence 1.0:
  https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
  https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
  Overture Maps Foundation: https://overturemaps.org

This derived database is itself made available under the ODbL 1.0. It was
produced by tools/osm_buildings in github.com/ZRosserMcIntosh/Aviation-Dynamics
(tile.py --ml: footprints reprojected to EPSG:31983 less the site origin,
simplified to 0.3 m; see index.json). Each .bin.gz is a gzipped AVB1 block.
