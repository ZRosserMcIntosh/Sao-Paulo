The building tiles in this folder (b_<i>_<j>.bin, tall.bin, index.json) are
derived from OpenStreetMap building footprints and heights, taken from the
Overture Maps buildings theme (release 2026-10-21.0; OpenStreetMap-sourced
features only).

  Data (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database
  Licence 1.0: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
  https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
  Overture Maps Foundation: https://overturemaps.org

This derived database is itself made available under the ODbL 1.0. It was
produced by tools/osm_buildings in github.com/ZRosserMcIntosh/Aviation-Dynamics
(footprints reprojected to EPSG:31983 less the site origin, simplified to
0.3 m, heights from the OSM height / building:levels tags, or estimated from
the footprint where neither is mapped; see index.json).
