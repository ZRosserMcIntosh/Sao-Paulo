Subscribers ask for something absurd in the comments, and it goes into the Destruction lab as a playable scenario. Every scenario here is fictional and runs in your browser.

The rule: whatever a challenge teaches us about the physics goes back into the main Destruction Dynamics engine, so the regular tower falls better too.

Day1

The indestructible cheese

“A giant block of cheese in the center forever (make it indestructible pls)”

requested by @Fartman32

An 85-storey tower tips into the Atlantic and lands on a 100 m wedge of Swiss, cut from the wheel, holes and all. The cheese does not move.

  • Engine lesson: floors now collide with convex static obstacles and release from the authored fall at the first contact.
  • Engine lesson: slab joints break by the force they carry, and contacts get a velocity pass, so the wreck settles without bouncing.
  • Engine lesson: floor keyframes stream in from a worker, so playback starts in milliseconds.
